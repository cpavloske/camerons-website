About

I'm an engineer and proud Aggie, with an electrical engineering degree from Texas A&M and a career spanning enterprise AV, camera and sensor systems, and IT architecture. As a Principal Architect at one of the world's largest enterprises, I spent years designing and deploying collaboration and camera systems at scale, including putting camera streams to work beyond physical security to power AI-driven operational use cases. I know what it's like to inherit a fragmented environment and be expected to make it work, because I've done it.

Today, I help organizations carry those same systems forward. Technology moves fast, and yesterday's solutions get left behind. My job is to help teams get ahead of that curve, choosing forward-looking platforms built for what's next that fit cleanly into their broader IT architecture and the collaboration tools their people actually rely on.
